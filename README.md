《生命無限無疆》為郭實獵（Karl Friedrich August Gützlaff，1803–1851）所撰，道光十八年（1838）由新嘉坡堅夏書院刊行，是早期漢語基督教短篇敘事。開端以「在廣州府有一人家」起筆，寫經商致富、好宴飲娛樂的李令，並以家貧的鄰人劉岱對照其巨富，又述李令病後酒友不來問候。收束處出現「新天地」「常生如何可得之乎」「信服耶穌」等語，將永生與信耶穌相連，末段由眾人說起對死亡的畏懼，並問何不甘心悅服救主。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 428712522 為底本，共取 14 幅固定影像，其中 10 幅含正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，274 項比對中保留 115 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Sheng ming wu xian wu jiang (生命無限無疆) is a short early Chinese-language Christian narrative by Karl Friedrich August Gützlaff (郭實獵, 1803–1851), printed in Singapore in 1838 (Daoguang 18) by the Jianxia Shuyuan (堅夏書院). It opens with the words "in Guangzhou Prefecture there was a household" and introduces Li Ling, who grew rich through trade and loves feasting and entertainment. It contrasts him with his poor neighbor Liu Dai and notes that after Li Ling falls ill, his drinking companions do not come to ask after him. Its closing columns use phrases such as "new heaven and earth," "how may everlasting life be obtained," and "believing in Jesus" to link eternal life with faith in Jesus. The dialogue closes with the group voicing its fear of death and asking why they should not willingly submit to the Savior.

The catalogue identifies Karl Friedrich August Gützlaff (郭實獵) as the author of the historical work. This dataset uses 14 fixed images, including 10 pages with main text, from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 428712522. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 115 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 274 項比對、115 項未解及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。
